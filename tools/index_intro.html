How to use this guide

This guide covers the course from Lecture 5 (Inflation and Monetary Policy) onwards: the microeconomics block (supply and demand, elasticity, consumer choice, the firm, market structures) and the two Welfare Economics classes. The lecture slides only list topics, so every model here is worked out in full: each supply–demand, cost, Phillips-curve or game-theory result is derived step by step, then checked with a numerical example whose numbers match the diagrams.

Colour code for the boxes
  • Key ideas: definitions and results to memorise.
  • Derivation: the full algebra or calculus behind a result.
  • Worked example: the derivation applied to numbers.
  • India focus: South Asian applications, matching the CORE South Asia edition.
  • Exam tip: common mistakes and how questions are usually framed.

How to revise with it

For each chapter: (1) read the Key-ideas boxes; (2) redo every derivation on paper without looking; (3) attempt the practice questions at the end before opening the answers; (4) redraw each figure from memory, labelling axes, curves, equilibrium points and shaded areas. Examiners give marks for correctly labelled diagrams and for stating the assumptions behind each model.
